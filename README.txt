1. ORIGINS
================================================================================

This application is the end product of a five-stage reduction documented in
spirituality_psychotherapy_analysis.txt:

STAGE 1 — CORPUS. Eleven academic documents on spirituality and psychotherapy
were categorized by application type, method, and administration agent. Only
two (Plante's I-SIT papers) contained operationalized Ignatian technique; the
rest ranged from Catholic-framework theory to secular meta-discussion.

STAGE 2 — PHILOSOPHIES. The corpus reduced to five philosophical strands and
one unresolved fault line: is spirituality in therapy a TOOL to be extracted
and tested, or a WORLDVIEW that cannot be instrumentalized without losing
what made it effective?

STAGE 3 — MECHANICS. Religion stripped away, the clinical encounter reduced
to six operations: attention training, sorting, decoupling, simulation, value
anchoring, and the asymmetric relational substrate. Thesis: every tradition is
a branding wrapper around these identical operations.

STAGE 4 — GEOMETRY. The operations were mapped onto a 3D coordinate system
following the PAD dimensional model (Mehrabian & Russell, 1974): X = valence,
Y = arousal, Z = dominance, each scaled -10 to +10. Diagnostic states became
coordinates; health became a region plus mobility, not a corner.

STAGE 5 — INSTRUMENT. The geometry was implemented as an interactive Android-
oriented HTML application, iterated three times:
  v1: the grapher (matrix-green, touch controls, point editor)
  v2: three lenses (Ignatian / Scientific / Machine) over one shared engine,
      per-lens names and notes, home-region ellipsoid, save/load
  v3: groups & chains (covariance territory ellipsoids, directed trajectories,
      Monte Carlo octant volumes, overlap statistics)

The three-lens architecture is the Stage 3 thesis made executable: switch the
lens and the identical data renders in a different justification vocabulary.
The Ignatian lens is anchored in the Spiritual Exercises (composed 1522-1524):
the 3D view itself is framed as Ignatius's "composition of place," the axes as
consolation/desolation, fervor/aridity, and freedom/attachment, with the Rules
for Discernment of Spirits embedded in the seeded points. The Scientific lens
is anchored in the PAD literature, the affect circumplex (Russell, 1980),
psychological flexibility (Kashdan & Rottenberg, 2010), and affect labeling
(Lieberman et al., 2007). The Machine lens shows the room.

3. THE SPOKEN SCRIPT
   Agent-to-subject wordplay for the suspected innocent
================================================================================

CASE: The subject is distressed because others regard them with suspicion
despite their genuine goodwill. Goal: equanimity — the others' verdicts cease
to command the subject's internal state, without sliding into numbness or
contempt.

The script follows the five waypoints. For each, the agent's lines are given
in three registers — the same operation, three vocabularies. The register
should match the subject's own framework; do not mix registers mid-session.

GENERAL DELIVERY NOTES
  - Slow. Every line below is followed by silence. The silence is doing the
    work; the words only aim the attention.
  - Never argue with the subject's account of events. The facts of who
    suspects whom are not the material. The displacement is the material.
  - The agent discloses nothing, evaluates nothing about the subject's
    character, and reacts to no disclosure with surprise. The relational
    substrate IS the intervention; the script rides on it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAYPOINT 1 — THE STING (-6, +5, -6)
Operation: ATTENTION TRAINING — locate the state precisely.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MACHINE REGISTER:
  "Before we do anything about it — where are you, exactly, right now?
   Not 'bad.' Bad is a region, not a point. When the look landed — what
   dropped, what spiked, what disappeared?"
  (wait)
  "So: it hurt — that's the drop. You went on watch — that's the spike.
   And the verdict felt like it was theirs to give — that's what
   disappeared. Three readings. You just located yourself. A located
   state is already a smaller state."

IGNATIAN REGISTER:
  "Let us begin where Ignatius begins — by composing the place. Close your
   eyes and stand again in the moment the look landed. Do not defend
   yourself yet. Only see it."
  (wait)
  "Now name the wound honestly. To be suspected while innocent is an old
   station — Ignatius stood in it himself, before the inquisitors at
   Alcalá, at Salamanca, at Paris. He did not pretend it was nothing.
   Neither will you. What, exactly, is wounded?"

SCIENTIFIC REGISTER:
  "I want to separate two columns before we go further. Column one: what
   you observed — a look, a tone, a seating choice. Column two: what you
   inferred — what you believe it meant. Give me column one only."
  (wait)
  "Notice the inference column is doing all the damage, and notice it
   contains no data you collected — only data you generated. Naming the
   components — the hurt, the vigilance, the helplessness — measurably
   lowers their intensity. We just did that."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAYPOINT 2 — THE SPIRAL (-7, +7, -8)
Operation: SORTING — classify thoughts as vectors, not facts.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MACHINE REGISTER:
  "Now the loop. You replay the looks, you read their minds, you rehearse
   a defense for a trial that never convenes. Here is the only question I
   want you to ask of any thought in the loop — not 'is it true?' but
   'where does it carry me?' 'They all think I'm rotten' — plot it. Where
   does that one land you?"
  (wait)
  "Further down, further out of control. Then that is its classification,
   whatever its truth-feeling. And note: the feeling of certainty is
   strongest exactly here, at the bottom of the spiral. Certainty is a
   symptom at this coordinate, not a signal."

IGNATIAN REGISTER:
  "Hear how the desolation argues. It speaks like a prosecutor: 'they see
   what you really are.' Now — the Fifth Rule, and I want it in your
   bones: in desolation, never revise a judgment made in consolation.
   You knew your own goodwill last week, in the light. Has the darkness
   presented new evidence — or only new darkness?"
  (wait)
  "Then do not re-litigate your character at midnight. And the Eighth
   Rule: this passes. It is lying to you when it swears it will not."

SCIENTIFIC REGISTER:
  "Two cognitive patterns are running: mind-reading — inferring their
   hostile thoughts without access to their thoughts — and
   personalization — taking their suspicion as a fact about you. Consider
   the alternative model: their suspicion is a function of THEIR base
   rates. Their history, their priors, their incomplete information.
   Whose variables are those?"
  (wait)
  "Theirs. Data about the observer is not data about the observed. The
   rumination itself is what's maintaining your arousal — the loop is the
   pathology now, not the trigger."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAYPOINT 3 — THE TURN (-2, +2, 0)
Operation: DECOUPLING — the trigger fires; the displacement shrinks.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MACHINE REGISTER:
  "We are not going to stop the glance from landing. We are going to
   shrink what it moves. Next time it lands: one breath. Then the label —
   say it inwardly, word for word: 'a glance is a glance — their
   inference, not my essence.' Then nothing. No argument, no flinch, no
   exit. Let the wave pass through an object that does not move."
  (wait)
  "You will not be calm yet. Calm is not the milestone. The milestone is
   this: still uncomfortable — no longer commanded. That is the hinge of
   the whole trajectory."

IGNATIAN REGISTER:
  "Now agere contra — act against the pull. The desolation counsels you:
   withdraw from them, defend yourself, grow cold. You will do the
   opposite — be openly, simply good toward them, precisely now. Not to
   win the verdict. The verdict was never the prize."
  (wait)
  "And here is the paradox at the heart of the Foundation: you regain
   your freedom by releasing the demand to be vindicated. Honor or
   dishonor — the free soul is poised before both. Whose hands does your
   goodwill rest in — theirs, or yours?"

SCIENTIFIC REGISTER:
  "The technique is defusion. Instead of 'they distrust me,' you say:
   'I am having the thought that they distrust me.' Say it now, in full."
  (wait)
  "Feel the inch of distance that opened? The thought just became an
   object you hold instead of a lens you look through. Expect oscillation
   — the curve back to baseline is not a straight line, and a bad
   afternoon is a data point, not a verdict."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAYPOINT 4 — STEADY (+3, 0, +5)
Operations: SIMULATION + VALUE ANCHORING — rehearse, and anchor conduct
to values rather than verdicts.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MACHINE REGISTER:
  "Now we install the destination. Tell me who you are when no one is
   grading you — three behaviors, not adjectives."
  (wait)
  "Those are your anchor coordinates. From now on conduct is calibrated
   against them, not against faces. Their suspicion may persist — it no
   longer sets your position. And we maintain this by rehearsal: tonight,
   eyes closed, run the next encounter. See the look. Feel the small
   flare. Stay in the body. Respond as the anchored self. Visit the
   coordinate until it has no teeth."

IGNATIAN REGISTER:
  "In the contemplation tonight, compose the place: the room, the faces,
   the look. Let the sting come — and remain. Respond in imagination as
   the person God already knows you to be. This is how the saint
   rehearsed: in the imagination first, with grace, before the event."
  (wait)
  "And test the peace you find there, by the Fourth Rule of the Second
   Week: trace where it ends. True consolation ends in charity toward
   the very people who suspect you. If it ends anywhere else, bring it
   back to me and we will discern it together."

SCIENTIFIC REGISTER:
  "Maintenance phase. Two components. One: values-congruent behavior —
   we've specified the behaviors; the schedule is daily, audience-blind.
   Two: imaginal rehearsal — scheduled, controlled exposure to the
   evaluative scenario, which prevents re-sensitization. The outcome
   variable is not their opinion. Their opinion is uncontrolled. Your
   displacement-per-trigger is controlled, measurable, and shrinking.
   That is the number we track."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAYPOINT 5 — UNSHAKEN (+4, -1, +7)
Operation: CALIBRATION CHECK — detachment without deafness.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MACHINE REGISTER:
  "Last point, and it comes with a warning. You asked for their opinions
   to mean nothing. Careful what you optimize for. 'No verdict commands
   my state' — that's health. 'No signal reaches me at all' — that's a
   sensor failure wearing strength's clothes, and it lives next door to
   the numbness coordinate. So: keep the antenna. Change what it's tuned
   to. Who are the two or three people whose correction should still
   reach you?"

IGNATIAN REGISTER:
  "Indiferencia is freedom from the verdict — never coldness toward the
   people. The same Foundation that releases you from their dishonor
   binds you to their good. And one discernment to carry with you: if
   this peace ever curdles into 'they are beneath my notice,' examine the
   movement's end — that is pride wearing detachment's clothes. The truly
   free man can still be corrected by a friend. Only the proud man
   cannot."

SCIENTIFIC REGISTER:
  "The adaptive endpoint is differentiated social referencing — high
   dominance with selective, not absent, sensitivity to evaluation. Total
   insensitivity removes error-correction and predicts poor outcomes. The
   weighting function: feedback weighted by source reliability and
   goodwill. A stranger's suspicion on bad priors: near zero. A friend's
   concern: still registers. And note the slightly negative arousal at
   this coordinate — correct. This is a restful state, not an excited
   one. We re-measure after the next incident and compare displacement."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLOSING LINE (all registers, verbatim or adapted):
  "Their suspicion was never evidence about you. Your recovery time is.
   And that number belongs entirely to you."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

NOTE ON USE: this is a worked example of structure and register, not a
treatment protocol. It pairs with the app by plotting each waypoint live with
the subject — the grid serves as the shared "composition of place" while the
words above aim the attention. For a person in real and persistent distress,
the script is an adjunct to, not a substitute for, a qualified human.

4. USER MANUAL — state_space_v3_groups.html
================================================================================

REQUIREMENTS
  Any modern Android browser (Chrome recommended); also works on desktop.
  Internet connection on first load (three.js loads from a CDN). Open the
  file directly — no install, no server.

SCREEN LAYOUT
  Top two-thirds: the 3D grid. Bottom third: the control panel (scrollable).
  A HUD in the top-left names the current lens and axes.

GESTURES (on the 3D view)
  One finger drag .......... rotate the grid around the focus point
  Two-finger pinch ......... zoom in/out
  Two-finger drag .......... pan the view left/right/up/down
  (Desktop: mouse-drag rotates, scroll wheel zooms.)

THE GRID
  Axes run -10 to +10. X = valence, Y = arousal (vertical), Z = dominance —
  renamed per lens (see LENSES). The translucent ellipsoid near (+4, 0, +5)
  is the home region: TARGET / INDIFERENCIA / SET-POINT depending on lens.
  Each point casts a faint drop-line to the floor for depth reading.

THE LENS BAR (top of panel)
  ◈ LENS button ............ opens/closes the lens drawer
  ⇩ SAVE ................... downloads the entire workspace as a .json file
  ⇧ LOAD ................... imports a previously saved .json

THE LENS DRAWER
  Three lens cards — tap to switch the entire instrument:
    ✠ IGNATIAN ... amber/serif; axes become Consolation, Fervor, Freedom;
        seeded points rename (Desolation, Acedia, False Consolation...);
        notes prompts become discernment questions; the about-text explains
        the grounding in the Spiritual Exercises and Rules of Discernment.
    △ SCIENTIFIC ... blue/sans; strict PAD terminology; about-text cites
        the source literature and states the model's limits.
    ▣ MACHINE ... matrix green; the operations without the story.
  Four global toggles: AXIS LABELS, HOME REGION, AMBIENT SPIN, DROP LINES.
  Below them, the ABOUT text for the active lens.
  NOTE: point names and notes are stored PER LENS. Editing a name in the
  Ignatian lens does not change its Machine name — the same state can be
  notated in each vocabulary. Switch lenses and watch the seeds rename.

THE POINT EDITOR
  ◀ / ▶ .................... cycle through points (counter shows position)
  Name field ............... rename the current point (per lens)
  + ADD .................... new point at the origin
  ✕ ........................ delete current point (also removed from groups)
  COORDINATES .............. number box + slider per axis, -10 to +10
  COLOR .................... Hue / Saturation / Lightness sliders + swatch
  SIZE ..................... scale slider (0.1 - 2.0)
  SHAPE .................... sphere, cube, tetrahedron, octahedron, cone, torus
  NOTES .................... free text, stored per lens
  The selected point pulses with a wireframe halo in the 3D view.

THE GROUPS SECTION (below the point editor; scroll down)
  Called GROUPS/REGIONS, MOVEMENTS/TERRITORIES, or CLUSTERS/CONFIDENCE
  REGIONS depending on lens. Same nav pattern: ◀ ▶ / + ADD / ✕.

  ⊕ ASSIGN THIS POINT ...... adds the currently selected POINT to the
                             currently selected GROUP (becomes ⊖ REMOVE when
                             already a member). A point may join any number
                             of groups.
  ◀ POS / POS ▶ ............ move the current point earlier/later in the
                             group's order (matters when CHAIN is on)
  CHAIN .................... treat member order as a sequence: draws a
                             directed path with arrowheads — one state
                             leading to the next. This is how to plot a
                             trajectory like the suspected-innocent script.
  TERRITORY ................ show/hide the group's ellipsoid
  HUE ...................... the group's color
  SPREAD k·σ ............... ellipsoid size: semi-axes are k times the
                             standard deviation along each principal
                             direction (1.0 ≈ the 1σ cloud, 2.0+ approaches
                             a 95%-style region)

  THE ELLIPSOID is the covariance region of the member points: centered on
  their mean, tilted along the cloud's principal directions. One member
  renders as a small sphere; a straight-line chain renders as a cigar.
  Ellipsoids draw additively — WHERE TWO TERRITORIES OVERLAP, THE
  INTERSECTION GLOWS BRIGHTER. In the Ignatian lens: where two spirits
  contend for the same ground.

  THE STATS BLOCK (auto-recomputes ~quarter second after any change):
    REGION CENTER ....... the territory's center. Labeled per the honesty
                          caveat: this is the center of ground covered,
                          NOT an average state — a journey's midpoint may
                          lie at coordinates never occupied.
    TOTAL VOLUME ........ ellipsoid volume in grid-units cubed (gu³)
    OCTANT SHARE ........ the eight (±X, ±Y, ±Z) octants, with the percent
                          and absolute volume of the territory in each,
                          tagged with the octant's diagnostic meaning in
                          the current lens — e.g. (-,+,-) reads
                          "panic·anxiety zone" / "agitation·scruples" /
                          "fear-range".
    OVERLAP ............. for each other group: what fraction of THIS
                          territory lies inside that one.
    Figures come from ~12,000 Monte Carlo samples; treat them as
    descriptive, good to about the nearest percent.

SAVE / LOAD
  ⇩ SAVE writes points, groups, per-lens text, and the active lens to a
  dated .json. ⇧ LOAD restores it. Files from v2 (including
  unjustly_suspected_trajectory.json) import cleanly; rebuild any chains
  by creating a group and assigning the points in order.
  IMPORTANT: the app holds nothing between sessions on its own — closing
  the tab without saving discards changes.

SUGGESTED WORKFLOW (one self-tracking session)
  1. Pick the lens that matches your vocabulary today.
  2. + ADD a point; place it where you actually are, not where you should be.
  3. Name it, color it, write the note (the writing is the Examen / the
     affect-labeling — it is not decoration).
  4. Assign it to a dated group ("JUNE", "THE WORK SITUATION"). Toggle CHAIN
     if the points form a sequence.
  5. Watch the territory: where does its volume sit in the octant table?
     Is it drifting toward the home region over weeks, or away?
  6. SAVE before closing.

5. THE DATA FORMAT (for hand-editing or generating files)
================================================================================

{
  "lens": "machine" | "ignatian" | "scientific",
  "nextPtId": <int>, "nextGrpId": <int>,
  "points": [{
     "id": <int>, "x": -10..10, "y": -10..10, "z": -10..10,
     "h": 0..360, "s": 0..100, "l": 0..100, "size": 0.1..2,
     "shape": "sphere"|"cube"|"tetra"|"octa"|"cone"|"torus",
     "lensText": {
        "machine":   {"name": "...", "notes": "..."},
        "ignatian":  {"name": "...", "notes": "..."},   // optional
        "scientific":{"name": "...", "notes": "..."}    // optional
     }}],
  "groups": [{
     "id": <int>, "name": "...", "hue": 0..360,
     "ordered": true|false,        // true = chain
     "show": true|false,           // ellipsoid visibility
     "k": 1.0..2.5,                // spread multiplier
     "members": [<point ids, in chain order>] }]
}

Missing lensText entries fall back to "machine". Missing ids are assigned
on import.

6. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & CAUTIONS
================================================================================

TECHNICAL
  - No automatic persistence: SAVE before closing.
  - First load requires a connection (CDN); afterward the page can be kept
    open offline.
  - Lens fonts use system stacks; exact appearance varies slightly by device.
  - Chains render as straight segments, not curves.
  - Octant tags assume the standard axis semantics; if you repurpose the
    axes, the tag tables in each lens config are where to edit.

CONCEPTUAL — carried over from the foundational analysis, deliberately
  - The axes are not orthogonal in real organisms; the space is not
    Euclidean; a person is a distribution, not a point.
  - Health is a region plus mobility — fixation anywhere, including the
    home region, is pathology. Optimizing toward (+10,+10,+10) is the
    mania corner, not success.
  - The grid is a navigation aid, not the territory. Treating it as
    literal — optimizing a human toward a coordinate — reproduces the
    instrumentalization problem the source analysis flagged.
  - This is a self-reflection instrument and a worked design study. It is
    not a diagnostic device and not therapy. Persistent or severe distress
    belongs with a qualified human, whichever lens they happen to speak.
